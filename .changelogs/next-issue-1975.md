- **[issue-1975] Internal: the market data service is split into its four modules** — `src/market-data-service.js` (2,358 lines) now holds only the passive ticker / metrics service and its wiring (618 lines). The timer tracker and order mailbox are in `order-mailbox.js`; stopped-fund fill ingestion, cancelled-order settlement and the partial / FILLED / cancel retry chains are in `stopped-fund-order-tracker.js` (`createStoppedFundOrderTracker({...})`, taking its collaborators as a dependency bag); the per-fund service map with start / stop / fence is in `market-data-registry.js` (`createMarketDataRegistry({...})`). These are moves, not rewrites: the start de-dup and stop-generation guard that keeps two writers off one fill ledger, the retry budgets (unbounded on Coinbase, six hours on Gemini and Crypto.com), fill ingestion and the stopped-fund status cadence are unchanged, and `market-data-service` still exports every name it did. The unused `setOnOrderFill` hook, which only tests called, is removed. No behaviour change for a running or stopped fund.
