- **[issue-1959] The recovered-row growth chain and the stale-order timer now respect the backup fence** — `extendBody` (and the start/recalc chain that grows bodies from recovered buy rows) parks at the engine's backup fence before cancelling and re-placing a body TP. Each chain run is tracked in its own context, so a parked extend no longer stalls the backup drain for 30 s and refuses the snapshot. A stale-entry timeout that fires during a hold waits for release, then re-reads the order before cancelling, so its cancel and the known-filled-size save that protects a partial fill are no longer split across the backup window.
