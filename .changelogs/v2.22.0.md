# Release v2.22.0

Released: 2026-07-04

## Highlights

- **Operator visibility into paused buying.** The dashboard now surfaces when a fund's buys are paused, with an operator action to reset the cycle instead of waiting for it to clear on its own.
- **More accurate order and fill accounting.** Cancelled orders with partial fills now report the correct filled size, ambiguous "unknown" Coinbase order placements are reconciled instead of stuck, and non-idempotent order POSTs no longer get blind-retried into a double-place.
- **Safer take-profit and SAFE-mode handling.** Fills route correctly when a SAFE-mode cancel is refused, and the TP mutex now guards against duplicate take-profit orders.
- **P&L and cost-basis fixes across cycles.** Fixed a partially-filled TP body incorrectly folding into the buy-fill merge path, a capital-growth double-apply on crash restart, dropped fees on synthetic fills, and P&L/holdback being summed per partial fill row instead of once per order.
- **Backtest and optimizer accuracy.** Backtests now debit the buy-side fee from the cash ledger, optimizer results are labeled by actual candle coverage, and same-interval sell proceeds no longer improperly fund same-interval buys.
- **Market data correctness.** Candles are sorted oldest-first before metrics are computed, 24h volume now flows from the ticker into market state, and in-progress candles are no longer persisted (upsert to the boundary bucket instead).
- **Hardened external fetches.** Sentinel feed URLs are validated against SSRF with a capped screenshot request body size, and the UpDown AI fetch now validates redirects, strips credentials, and closes a DNS-rebinding TOCTOU gap.
- **Flat-engine reliability.** Fixed a SAFE-mode deadlock, dead REST-error triggers, NaN volatility baseline, and mishandling of flash moves across gaps; the Fibonacci sell-consolidation state machine was repaired.
- **UI polish.** Regime charts dedupe and sort their price/ATR series, the log stream re-subscribes after a socket reconnect, and several UI panels guard against unhandled fetch rejections and stray input edge cases.
- **Multi-exchange and multi-fund correctness.** Four exchange-adapter correctness fixes landed, and sync-fills now threads the actual fund pair instead of hardcoding BTC.
- **Test coverage.** Added integration tests covering the merge↔fill concurrency surface.
