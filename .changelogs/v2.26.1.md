# Release v2.26.1

Released: 2026-09-24

## Highlights

- **Ladder rebuilds keep capital reserved while fills settle.** A partial rung fill that is still being booked cannot be mistaken for free capital, so replacement orders do not over-allocate the fund.
- **Overlapping cycle resets stay in the right cycle.** Queued resets no longer double-count completed cycles or optimizer samples, and buys owned by an open body are carried into the cycle that will close them.
- **Split sell bookings remain one audit record.** Repeated bookings for the same sell order now aggregate their quantities, proceeds, fees, cost basis, profit and reserves while retries remain idempotent.
- **Manual imports recover existing positions after a crash.** A persisted body is found before later fills are reconciled, so an imported buy grows the correct position instead of creating a duplicate.
- **Historical fill scans are paced and bounded.** Crypto.com and Gemini avoid unbounded or bursty history walks and surface incomplete results for retry instead of silently accepting partial data.

## Fill accounting and cycle safety

- **[issue-776] Reserve polled partial fills during ladder rebuilds** — A ladder sweep now reports partial-fill reservations and re-reads the fill ledger before sizing replacement orders, reserving only the tranche that has not been durably booked yet.
- **[issue-782] Keep queued ladder resets on the correct cycle** — A reset that discovers another reset already turned over its captured cycle now becomes a no-op for cycle and optimizer accounting, while an owed reset preserves buys owned by bodies that remain open.
- **[issue-786] Aggregate repeated closed-trade bookings** — A second booking for one sell order updates its existing closed-trade record with the newly booked execution, including sell totals, cost basis, realized profit, holdback reserves and buy links; a replay remains unchanged.

## Import and recovery

- **[issue-788] Recover manual-import body ownership before reconciling fills** — Manual buy import now checks the fill ledger and persisted position bodies before deciding whether to create a new body, allowing a post-crash fill to extend the body that already owns the order.

## Exchange reliability

- **[issue-787] Pace and bound historical fill scans** — Crypto.com history pages are paced and capped at 64 pages per call, incomplete scans are marked for retry, and Crypto.com/Gemini retry waits are excluded from health latency so a slow backfill does not falsely trigger a health response.

## Upgrade requirements

- Stop the configured Critical Mass processes before updating the deployed checkout. Install root and admin dependencies with `npm ci`, rebuild with `npm run build`, and restart the PM2 processes.
- This patch release adds no configuration migration. After restarting, confirm that ladder rebuilds, cycle totals and imported fills look correct in the dashboard and logs.
- The private admin package and Umbrel packaging keep their own versions.
