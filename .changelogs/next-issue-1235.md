- **[issue-1235] The hourly fund-state backup no longer trips SAFE mode on every fund** — A restarted regime engine builds a fresh health monitor that starts with `wsConnected: false`, so its first health tick (before the WebSocket reconnected) raised `ws_disconnected`, entered SAFE, cancelled every resting entry bid and fired a critical `safe_mode` alert once an hour (and on every boot/deploy). The health monitor now waits `wsConnectGraceMs` (default 30000, next to `safeRecoveryMs`) for the first connect before treating a missing feed as lost; a feed that never connects still trips SAFE after the grace, and a drop after a successful connect still trips immediately.
