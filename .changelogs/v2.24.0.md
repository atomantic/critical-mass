# Release v2.24.0 — UpDown paper positions and safer reconciliation

Released: 2026-09-09

## Highlights

- UpDown now presents Open, Add, Hold, and Close decisions for perpetual longs, with a paper-position P&L monitor and replayable fill history.
- Partial sells remain tracked until terminal exchange status and final fills are verified. Offline recovery preserves unresolved orders for retry.
- Operator sign-in is optional, with persistent password configuration through the Gateway page.

## Trading and analytics

- Batch buy-to-sell ledger links during large roll-ups to avoid engine stalls and gateway timeouts.
- Preserve take-profit protection when cancellation fails, align minimum-size checks with exchange increment rounding, and retain holdback at floating-point boundaries.
- Make UpDown scoring causal and fail closed, with cost-aware walk-forward validation, stricter trade-expression checks, and consistent live/historical scorecard interpretation.
- Refresh stale daily candles, reuse historical fill groups and candle labels, and rank streaming optimizer results consistently with final results.
- Validate fund pairs and aggressiveness presets consistently. Preserve rejected trade-history edits and recover dashboard controls after failed requests.

## Dashboard and operation

- Load dashboard routes on demand, improve mobile layouts and touch targets, and label configuration controls for accessibility.
- Keep operator credentials in private data storage, harden password enrollment, and preserve authentication across visits.
- Improve structured trading and lifecycle logs while retaining operator-facing messages.
- Update dependencies and clarify native PM2 installation requirements.

## Upgrade notes

- The gateway, development dashboard, and engine IPC ports moved to **5570–5574**. Update bookmarks, `GATEWAY_URL`, `CORS_ORIGINS`, and reverse-proxy settings that pin the previous ports.
- Operator sign-in is configured on the Gateway page; the password hash persists in `data/operator-auth.json` rather than an environment token.
- Paper-position results are simulations, not exchange fills.
- Follow the [versioned installation guide](https://github.com/atomantic/critical-mass/blob/v2.24.0/README.md#installation), [configuration instructions](https://github.com/atomantic/critical-mass/blob/v2.24.0/README.md#configuration), and [upgrade guide](https://github.com/atomantic/critical-mass/blob/v2.24.0/UPGRADE.md). Preserve existing data and credentials when upgrading.
