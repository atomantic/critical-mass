- **[issue-1025] Resume funds automatically after transient backup restart failures** — Scheduled backups now restart paused funds under automatic desired-running supervision, so a transient exchange error during the restart schedules a retry (deferred until maintenance ends) instead of leaving the fund stopped. Repeat `engine:backup-resume` calls report funds still pending recovery or blocked rather than claiming success, and a backup pause waits for any in-flight retry before capturing engines.
