# Release v2.29.1

Released: 2026-10-29

## Highlights

- **DCA sell safety.** Terminal partial sells are now booked in both DCA strategies, DCA sell ownership is persisted before placement intents are cleared, and a missing order lookup is treated as a refused sell retry rather than a free slot.
- **Fibonacci sell handling.** Unconfirmed Fibonacci sells stay owned instead of being replaced, sell execution is rechecked after cancellation, and earlier partial sales are recovered for cycles saved before cycle-wide tracking (with the legacy recovery limit documented).
- **DCA consolidation and fees.** Aborting a consolidation mid-cancel restores earlier cancellations (tolerating nullish rejections), and unavailable fees no longer become permanent zero fees.
- **Backup and restore integrity.** Unrestorable full backups no longer displace usable recovery points, late scorecard writes are drained before a restore is applied, conflicting legacy accounting copies are refused before an archive is applied, and recovery-buy sidecars are validated before snapshots are published or restored.

## Upgrade requirements

- No configuration migration is required. Follow the coordinated gateway and engine restart order in [UPGRADE.md](https://github.com/atomantic/critical-mass/blob/v2.29.1/UPGRADE.md).
- The private admin package and Umbrel manifests retain their separate version histories.
